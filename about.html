🌍 The Digital Ecosystem

Limazon is not just a game; it's a fully simulated digital society. Players start from nothing and navigate a complex, player-driven economy. Whether you choose to climb the corporate ladder, manipulate the virtual stock market, or orchestrate heists in the digital underground, your actions permanently shape the universe.

System Access & Authentication

Access to Limazon is strictly invite-only. To ensure seamless and secure access for our invited citizens, we utilize modern authentication standards including WebAuthn (Passkeys) and OAuth 2.0 via Google and GitHub.

Why do we ask for Google/GitHub login?
We use OAuth strictly as a secure key to your account. We only request basic profile access (your email address) to prevent duplicate accounts and securely verify your identity. No data is shared with third parties.
