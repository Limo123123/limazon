Limazon Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

1. About this Project

Limazon.Universe is a closed, invite-only, non-commercial multiplayer web game. This service is strictly private. All in-game currencies, clans, properties, and services have zero real-world value.

2. Data Collection (OAuth)

If you choose to log in using a third-party provider (like Google or GitHub), we request only the most basic information required for authentication:

  • Your email address (to uniquely identify your account and prevent duplicates).
  • Your public profile name (used as your default in-game username).

3. Data Usage & Protection

Your email address is securely stored in our private database and is used exclusively for authentication purposes and critical account recovery. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties, advertising networks, or analytics companies.

4. User Content

Any content uploaded or created within the game (text, images, videos, in-game chats) is stored on our servers to provide the multiplayer experience. Since this is a private project, server administrators reserve the right to delete any content or ban accounts that violate our community guidelines.

5. Right to Deletion

You have the right to delete your account at any time. A self-service deletion option is available in the account settings. Upon deletion, your account data, including your linked email address, will be permanently erased from our active databases.

Last updated: October 2026

Contact: If you have any questions, please contact the server administrator at tfl@toens.net.
