Privacy Policy & Terms

Limazon.Universe

1. About this Project

Limazon.Universe is a closed, invite-only, non-commercial multiplayer web game. This service is strictly private. All in-game currencies, properties, businesses, and services are fictional and possess zero real-world value.

2. Data Collection (OAuth)

If you choose to authenticate using a third-party provider (such as Google or GitHub), our application securely requests only the most basic information required for account creation and login:

  • Email Address: Used to uniquely identify your account, prevent duplicates, and serve as a fallback for account recovery.
  • Public Profile Name: Used solely to generate your initial in-game character alias.

3. Data Usage & Protection

Your email address is securely stored in our private database and is used exclusively for authentication purposes. We absolutely do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties, advertising networks, or analytics companies. Your data stays within the Limazon ecosystem.

4. User Content & Rules

Any content uploaded or created within the game (text, images, in-game chats, social media posts) is stored on our servers to provide the multiplayer experience. Since this is a private environment, server administrators reserve the right to delete any content or terminate accounts that violate our community guidelines or abuse the system.

5. Right to Deletion (GDPR)

You have full control over your data. A self-service "Delete Account" option is available directly within the game's account settings (Danger Zone). Executing this action will permanently and irreversibly erase your account, email address, linked OAuth IDs, and all associated game assets from our active databases.
